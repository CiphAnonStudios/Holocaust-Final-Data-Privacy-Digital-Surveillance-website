Your Data Is Being Watched

Every search, click, and app you use collects personal information. Data privacy and digital surveillance have become major human rights issues in today’s digital world.

Why This Matters

Companies and websites collect huge amounts of personal information every day. Most people do not realize how much of their online activity is being tracked.

Tracking

Apps and websites monitor searches, clicks, and location data.

Privacy

People can lose control over how their information is used online.

Security

Data leaks and cyber attacks can expose personal information.

Influence

Collected data can affect ads, recommendations, and online content.
