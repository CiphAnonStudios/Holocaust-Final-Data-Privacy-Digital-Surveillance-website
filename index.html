Your Data Is Being Watched

Every search, click, and app you use collects personal information. Data privacy and digital surveillance have become major human rights issues in today’s online world.

Learn More

Why This Matters

Many people do not realize how much of their information is collected online. Apps, websites, and social media platforms track user behavior every day.

Constant Tracking

Apps and websites monitor activity, searches, and location data.

Loss of Privacy

Personal information can be collected without full understanding or consent.

Security Risks

Data leaks and cyber attacks can expose private user information.

Targeted Influence

Collected data is used to influence ads, content, and recommendations.
