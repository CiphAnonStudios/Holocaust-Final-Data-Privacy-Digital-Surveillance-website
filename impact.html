The Impact of Data Privacy Issues

Data privacy affects millions of people every day. Many apps, websites, and social media platforms collect personal information without users fully understanding how much data is being tracked.

This can lead to loss of privacy, security risks, and misuse of personal information. Some companies use collected data to target ads, influence behavior, or share information with other businesses.

Teenagers and young adults are especially affected because they spend a lot of time online. Many people do not realize how much information they are sharing through apps, searches, and social media.

Why This Matters

Loss of Privacy

Personal information can be collected and stored without full permission.

Security Risks

Data leaks and hacking can expose sensitive information online.

Tracking

Apps and websites can track your location, searches, and online activity.

Influence

Companies can use data to influence what users see online.
