The IMPACT of Digital Surveillance

Digital surveillance and data collection affect people around the world every day. Personal information can be tracked, stored, shared, or even leaked without users fully understanding what is happening online.

How People Are Affected

Loss of Privacy

People may lose control over their personal information when apps and companies collect large amounts of data.

Data Breaches

Hackers and cyber attacks can expose passwords, locations, emails, and financial information.

Mental Effects

Constant tracking and online monitoring can make people feel unsafe or uncomfortable online.

Why Awareness Matters

Many people do not realize how much information is collected online every day. Spreading awareness helps people understand their digital rights and learn safer ways to protect themselves online.


Schools, organizations, and governments continue working to improve privacy protections and create stronger digital safety laws for the future.
