Your Data Is Being Tracked

Every click, search, and app you use generates data. Companies and organizations collect this information to analyze behavior, target ads, and influence decisions—often without full awareness or consent.

Why Data Privacy Matters

Constant Monitoring

Apps and websites track your behavior, location, and preferences continuously.

Commercial Use

Collected data is often sold or used for targeted advertising and profit.

Security Risks

Data breaches and leaks expose personal information to cybercriminals.

Loss of Control

Individuals have limited control over how their personal data is used.

The Bigger Issue

Digital surveillance has become a global concern. Governments and corporations have access to massive amounts of personal data, raising ethical questions about privacy, consent, and human rights in the digital age.

Understanding data privacy is the first step toward protecting your digital rights.
