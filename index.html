Your Data Isn’t Private

How companies track your information without you realizing it

What’s Happening?

Every time you use your phone, scroll social media, or search online, your data is being collected. Companies track what you like, where you go, and what you click. This creates serious concerns about privacy and your rights in the digital world.

Why This Matters

📱 Constant Tracking

Apps monitor your location, searches, and activity daily.

💰 Data is Sold

Your personal information is often sold to advertisers.

🎯 Targeted Ads

Ads are designed based on your behavior and interests.

🔓 Privacy Risks

Your data can be leaked or hacked without you knowing.

Did You Know?

Many apps collect data even when you’re not using them. Some track your location in the background, and others collect your contacts, messages, and browsing habits.
