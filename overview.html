Overview of Data Privacy

Data privacy and digital surveillance affect millions of people every day. Companies, governments, and apps collect personal information through websites, social media, phones, and online services. Many people do not realize how much of their information is stored, tracked, or shared online.

Main Issues

Surveillance

Governments and companies can monitor online activity, locations, and personal information.

Data Collection

Apps and websites gather huge amounts of user data for ads, tracking, and recommendations.

Privacy Risks

Personal information can be leaked, stolen, or shared without permission.

Why It Matters

Digital privacy is considered a human rights issue because everyone deserves control over their personal information. Without privacy protections, people can be tracked, influenced, or exposed online.

Many organizations and activists are working to improve online safety by creating stronger privacy laws, increasing awareness, and teaching people how to protect their information online.

As technology continues to grow, protecting digital privacy becomes more important for individuals, schools, businesses, and communities around the world.
