Your Data Isn’t Private

How companies track your information online without you realizing it

Every time you use your phone or go online, your personal data is being collected. Apps, websites, and social media platforms track your activity, location, and interests. This raises serious concerns about privacy and your rights in the digital world.

Quick Facts
