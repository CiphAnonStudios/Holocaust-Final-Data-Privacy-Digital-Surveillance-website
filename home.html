Your Data Is Not Private

Every time you go online, your data is being tracked, collected, and used. From social media to apps, companies gather personal information that raises serious concerns about privacy and human rights.

Learn More

What’s Happening?

Digital surveillance has become a normal part of everyday life. Companies collect data about your behavior, location, and interests to better understand and influence users. Most people don’t fully realize how much of their information is being tracked.

Why It Matters

Constant Tracking

Apps and websites monitor your activity, location, and behavior.

Data is Sold

Personal information is often shared or sold to advertisers.

Security Risks

Data breaches can expose sensitive personal information.

Loss of Privacy

Users have limited control over how their data is used.

Take Action

Protect your privacy by checking app permissions, limiting what you share online, and staying informed. Understanding your digital rights is the first step toward protecting them.

Raising awareness about digital privacy is essential in today’s world.
