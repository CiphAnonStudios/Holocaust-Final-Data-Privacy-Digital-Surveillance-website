Real Examples of Data Tracking

Data privacy issues happen every day, often without people realizing it. Many websites, apps, and social media platforms collect information about users and track online activity.

Targeted Ads

After searching for something online, ads related to that topic often appear on social media or other websites.

Location Tracking

Many apps collect location data in the background, even when the app is not being used.

Social Media Data

Social media platforms collect information about likes, searches, and user behavior.

Cookies

Websites use cookies to track activity and remember user behavior online.
