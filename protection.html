How to Protect Your Data

There are many simple ways to protect your personal information online. Learning how to stay safe can help reduce tracking, improve privacy, and keep your accounts secure.

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords that are hard to guess and avoid using the same password for every account.

Check App Permissions

Review what apps can access, like your location, camera, or contacts.

Turn Off Location

Disable location tracking when it is not needed to reduce data collection.

Be Careful Online

Avoid sharing too much personal information on social media and websites.

Take Action

Understanding data privacy is the first step toward protecting yourself online. By learning about digital surveillance and making safer choices, people can help protect their rights and spread awareness about this issue.
