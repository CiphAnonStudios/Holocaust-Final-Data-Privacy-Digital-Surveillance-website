Real-World Examples

Data privacy issues happen every day. These examples show how companies, apps, and governments collect or misuse personal information.

Examples of Data Privacy Issues

Data Breaches

Companies like social media platforms or stores can get hacked, exposing passwords, emails, and personal data.

Location Tracking

Apps track your exact location even when you are not using them, sometimes without clear permission.

Targeted Ads

Your searches and clicks are used to show personalized ads based on your behavior.

Real Situations

Social Media Tracking

Many social media apps collect data like what you like, watch, and search to build a profile about you.

Shopping Websites

Online stores track what you view and buy to predict what you might want next.

Free Apps

Some free apps make money by collecting and selling user data to advertisers.
