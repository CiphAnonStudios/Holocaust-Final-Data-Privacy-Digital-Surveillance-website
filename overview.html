What is Data Privacy?

Data privacy is about protecting your personal information online. This includes things like your location, messages, search history, and what you do on apps and websites.

When you use your phone or go online, companies collect this data. They use it to understand your behavior, show ads, and improve their services. Many people do not realize how much of their data is being collected every day.

Digital surveillance means that your activity is being watched or tracked. This can happen through apps, social media, and websites. Because of this, data privacy has become an important issue in today’s digital world.

Why You Should Care

Your personal data is valuable, and when it is collected without your full understanding, it can lead to problems. This includes losing control of your information, being tracked without knowing, and possible risks like data leaks or hacking.
